    How it works: data sources and methods

    Scores are built by a reproducible pipeline (python -m pipeline build) from World Bank, WHO, EF English Proficiency Index 2025, Global Peace Index 2026 and city climate data. Each indicator is clipped to its 5th–95th percentile and scaled to 0–1 across the {{ country_count }} countries. Hover a row in any score breakdown to see the exact source and year behind that number.

    FactorHow it's measured
    {{ icon(d.key) }} {{ d.label }}{{ d.method }}

    Machine learning, and where it is not used. Rankings are always the weighted sum above. ML fills a few missing data points (only where cross-validation shows it is reliable; marked “ML-imputed”), groups countries on the similarity map (PCA + k-means), learns slider weights from the 8-question quiz (pairwise preference model), and suggests a visa route from your prompt when the built-in vocabulary doesn't recognise one (chips marked with the chip icon). Sentence embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) match prompts and visas by meaning when they share no keywords (chips marked with the sparkle icon, and “meaning match” results). Each model's measured accuracy is in the project README.

    Confidence and “how to qualify”. The confidence line re-ranks countries in 400 what-if scenarios, nudging your weights and each data point by how reliable its source is. The “How to open more doors” section re-runs the visa rules with one thing changed (income, savings or a filter) to show exactly what would open more countries.

    Costs, timelines and rules. Visa fees, processing times, years to permanent residence, age limits and family rules come from official government pages, each linked on its visa card with the date it was checked. The eligibility rules themselves are data (data/rules.json), read by both the server and this page.

    Estimates and currencies. A value marked ≈ had no measurement for that country, so it was filled by a model, a regional median or an editorial estimate. Amounts are stored in US dollars; to show them in your currency we use ECB daily reference rates where there is one, otherwise the World Bank's yearly average official rate.

    Data version {{ data_version }}. Visa rules are curated by hand from official immigration portals and change often. Always confirm on the official site.
